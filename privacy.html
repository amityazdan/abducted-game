Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Grass Invaders is an online co-op game made by Amit Yazdan ("we"). This page explains what the game does with your data. Short version: there are no accounts, we don't sell anything about you, and voice chat is never recorded.

What the game stores

  • On your computer only: your settings (player name, colour, volume, graphics, key bindings). They never leave your device unless you join a game, where other players see your name and colour.
  • No accounts, no email, no payment data. If you bought the game on Steam, Steam handles the purchase under Valve's privacy policy; we don't receive your payment details.

Online play

  • Finding games: when you host or browse games, the game talks to our matchmaking server (Cloudflare Workers). It keeps a list of open rooms (room code, host's player name, player count, game version) in memory while the room is open. It doesn't keep logs of who played.
  • Peer-to-peer: the game itself runs directly between the players' computers (WebRTC). As in any peer-to-peer game, the other players' computers learn your IP address. To connect, the game uses public STUN servers (Cloudflare, Google), and when a direct connection isn't possible, a Cloudflare relay (TURN) that forwards the game traffic without storing it.
  • Voice chat: during a game, when you speak (open mic, the default) or while you hold the talk key (push-to-talk, in the settings), your microphone audio is sent live to the other players in your game. It is not recorded or stored by us or anyone we work with. macOS and Windows also let you deny the game microphone access entirely.
  • Update check: versions downloaded from our website check GitHub once in a while for a new version (the request contains no personal data). Steam versions are updated by Steam.

Children

Grass Invaders contains cartoon violence and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we don't keep personal data on our servers, there's usually nothing for us to delete. To remove everything locally, uninstall the game and delete its settings folder. For any privacy question or request, contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Changes
